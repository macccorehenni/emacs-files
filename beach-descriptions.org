
At the beach, the Ten, or rather, the Songwalkers, walked, each member with their own experiences, each with their own ideas and, truly, ideals, and some even with their own new bonds. Some were with the group since their humble beginnings as a team of singing, magic–learning students, some were part of groups not known until recently. But all had been prophesized to be a part of something bigger— a team, if you will, of those tasked with keeping the five realms of the Multi-world from losing all that made it unique, all that made it what it was. Each world, whether small or large, all had its fair share of Feybraid— the poison that corrupted all magic, prevented it from reaching those who meant good. But, as Iris, the leader of this group, walked on, she reflected on her dominion, and how, as far as she knew, how it was free of Feybraid.
